Overview

I am an Assistant Professor at the China Economics and Management Academy, Central University of Finance and Economics. I am an applied economist working at the intersection of innovation economics, strategy, and international business. My research examines how firms develop, reorganize, and derive value from technological capabilities, with particular interests in firm innovation strategy, knowledge spillovers, intellectual property, and sustainable innovation.

I received my Ph.D. in International Economics and Finance from Brandeis University in 2024, where I was advised by Gary H. Jefferson. Before that, I earned a B.S. in Economics, Mathematics and Finance from Renmin University of China.
